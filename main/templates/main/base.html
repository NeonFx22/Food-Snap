How FoodSnap Works

Culinary Computer Vision & Recipe Guide

01

Spatial HSV Decomposition

Photos are partitioned into a 4×4 spatial grid and converted into 80-dimensional color and saturation feature histograms.

02

Cosine Vector Matching

Vectors are compared against authentic reference food manifolds in milliseconds, ranking candidates by angular cosine similarity.

03

Step-by-Step Cooking Assistant

Interactive stage timers, ingredient prep checklists, and culinary tips guide you through cooking every recognized dish.

04

Nearby Restaurant Radar

Locate physical restaurants and eateries near you that serve the recognized dish with live OpenStreetMap & directions.
